8. Full Description
Kampus is the all-in-one inter-college event discovery, deadline management, student networking, and digital credentials platform designed for colleges and universities.

Whether you are looking to participate in hackathons, technical symposiums, cultural fests, sports tournaments, workshops, or academic conferences across different campuses, Kampus bridges the gap between students, faculty, and college administrators.

Key Benefits for Students:
- Explore Live Database Events: Access verified inter-college events stored dynamically in MongoDB Atlas with real-time sync.
- Smart Registration Tracking: Filter events by upcoming deadlines, categories, and eligibility. Expired events auto-archive so you never miss an active registration window.
- Cloudinary CDN Asset Delivery: High-resolution event banners, certificates, and student profile media loaded instantly through Cloudinary CDN.
- Exact Deadline Alarms & FCM Notifications: Receive background notifications and precise alerts for critical registration closing dates.
- Real-Time Team Finder: Connect with peers across colleges, discover teammates with complementary skill sets (coding, UI/UX, AI/ML), view verified certificates, and chat to form teams.
- Digital Student Portfolios: Build your academic profile, showcase your achievements, and manage verifiable certificates.

Key Benefits for Faculty and Administrators:
- Multi-Role Portal (Super Admin, College Admin, Dept Faculty): Granular permission hierarchy to create and verify events, manage student rosters, and publish official campus announcements.
- Event Publishing & Notification Broadcasts: Publish events with high-resolution imagery stored on Cloudinary CDN and broadcast instant FCM push notifications to registered students.
- Campus & Department Management: Maintain college directories, campus maps, department coordinators, and announcements.

9. Main Features
- Inter-College Event Discovery: Dynamic portal displaying technical, cultural, management, and sports events loaded live from MongoDB Atlas database.
- Cloud Image Integration: Cloudinary CDN integration for fast, reliable image uploading and optimization across all event, certificate, and profile media.
- Automated Deadline Filtration: Intelligent date tracking that displays active upcoming deadlines and filters out past events.
- Peer-to-Peer Team Finder: Dedicated directory to discover teammates filtered by skills, department, and event criteria.
- Real-Time Direct Messaging: In-app chat system for event participants and prospective team partners.
- FCM Push Notifications & Reminders: Instant notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging for newly published events and scheduled exact alarms for closing deadlines.
- Multi-Role Access Control: Specialized interfaces and administrative controls for Super Admins, College Admins, Department Faculty, and Students.
- Digital Certificates & Student Portfolios: Verified student profiles featuring bios, skills, past participations, and uploaded certificates.

28. Permissions used by the application
- Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
- Calendar / Alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM)
- Boot Receiver (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)
- Internet (android.permission.INTERNET)
- SMS (android.permission.SEND_SMS)

29. Explain why these permissions are required
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to deliver realtime FCM notifications to students regarding newly published college events, deadline reminders, and direct messages.
- SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: Required to schedule precise deadline alarms and calendar reminders even when the app is running in the background.
- RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required to re-register active deadline alarms when the user's device reboots.
- INTERNET: Required for secure communication with MongoDB Atlas cloud database, Cloudinary CDN media server, and FCM push servers.
- SEND_SMS: Used as an optional direct contact method with event coordinators.
