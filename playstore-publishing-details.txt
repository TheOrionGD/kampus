8. Full Description
Kampus is the all-in-one inter-college event discovery, deadline management, and student networking platform designed for colleges and universities.

Whether you are looking to participate in hackathons, technical symposiums, cultural fests, sports tournaments, or academic conferences across different campuses, Kampus bridges the gap between students and organizers.

Key Benefits for Students:
- Explore Verified Events: Access inter-college events organized across multiple universities and institutions with real-time updates.
- Smart Registration Tracking: Filter events by upcoming deadlines, categories, and eligibility. Events automatically update so you never miss a registration window.
- Exact Deadline Alarms: Set reminders and receive background notifications on critical registration dates.
- Real-Time Team Finder: Connect with peers across colleges, discover students with complementary skill sets (coding, design, management), view verified certifications, and chat in real-time to form competitive teams.
- Student Portfolios: Build your academic profile, showcase your achievements, and share certificates with the campus community.

Key Benefits for Faculty and Organizers:
- Event Publishing: Publish and manage college events, brochures, and external registration links with instant push notification delivery to all registered students.
- Campus Management: Maintain college directories, campus maps, department coordinators, and announcements.

9. Main Features
- Inter-College Event Discovery: Centralized portal displaying technical, cultural, management, and sports events from various universities.
- Automated Deadline Filtration: Intelligent date tracking that displays active upcoming deadlines and filters out expired events.
- Peer-to-Peer Team Finder: Dedicated matchmaking directory where students can find teammates filtered by skills and events.
- Real-Time Messaging: One-to-one messaging system for event participants and prospective team partners.
- Push Notifications and Reminders: Instant notifications for newly published events and scheduled exact alarms for closing deadlines.
- Student Profile and Certificate Showcase: Profile management featuring bio, skills, past participations, and verifiable certificates.
- Faculty and Admin Portal: Verification system for college coordinators to publish events, post announcements, and view registrations.

28. Permissions used by the application
- Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
- Calendar / Alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM)
- Boot Receiver (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)
- Internet (android.permission.INTERNET)
- SMS (android.permission.SEND_SMS)

29. Explain why these permissions are required
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to notify students about newly published college events, urgent deadline reminders, and direct messages from teammates.
- SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: Required to trigger exact-time deadline alerts and calendar reminders even when the app is in the background or killed.
- RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required to reschedule active deadline reminders and alarms when the user restarts their phone.
- INTERNET: Required to communicate with Firebase Cloud Messaging and MongoDB Atlas to fetch live event listings and team directory data.
- SEND_SMS: Used as an optional fallback for offline emergency contact with event coordinators.
