Privacy Policy

Learn how Kampus collects, protects, and handles user data for inter-college event discovery, team matching, and academic portfolios.

Application Version: 2.1.3 (Build 7) • Package: com.kampus.app • Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to Kampus ("we", "our", or "us"). Kampus is an inter-college event discovery, deadline management, student team finder, and academic credentials platform designed for students, faculty, and university administrators.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our mobile application and backend services.

2 Information We Collect

To provide a functional campus networking and event discovery experience, Kampus collects the following categories of data:

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, academic email address, college/university name, department, academic year, user role (Student, Faculty, College Admin, Super Admin), bio, and verified skills.
  • Photos & Media Storage: Profile photos, cover banners, project attachments, and verifiable certificate documents. All uploaded media is processed and stored securely via Cloudinary CDN.
  • Device & FCM Registration Tokens: Device identification tokens collected for Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver realtime push notifications regarding event updates, registration deadlines, and direct messages.
  • Application Activity: Event registrations, direct chat messages between team partners, deadline alarm reminders, and campus announcements.

3 How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected data strictly for providing and improving Kampus core features:

  • Event Discovery & Live Feeds: Presenting dynamic college events stored and synchronized in real-time via MongoDB Atlas cloud database.
  • Peer-to-Peer Team Finder: Enabling students to find teammates based on skill sets, department, and event participation.
  • Real-Time Notifications: Dispatching urgent registration closing alerts, calendar alarms, and event announcements via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Verifying administrative permissions for faculty and college administrators to post events and broadcast announcements.

4 Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

Kampus integrates industry-standard third-party SDKs and cloud infrastructure services to power its features:

Third-Party Service Purpose Data Handled
MongoDB Atlas Cloud Database Persistence & Realtime Sync User profiles, event documents, team requests
Cloudinary CDN Media Asset Storage & Delivery Uploaded profile pictures, event banners, certificates
Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Push Notification Delivery Device registration tokens, notification payloads
Coil Compose Image Cache & Async Display Transient image cache on client device

5 Android App Permissions

The application requests the following Android permissions to function properly:

  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to send realtime background notifications for upcoming event deadlines and direct messages.
  • android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: Used to schedule precise deadline alarms on your device.
  • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required to reschedule active deadline alarms after device reboots.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for database communication, Cloudinary media loading, and push notifications.

6 Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers or marketing networks.

Your profile information (Name, College, Skills, Bio, Certificates) is visible to other verified students and campus coordinators within the app directory to facilitate team matching and event participation.

7 Data Security & Account Deletion

We implement robust transport security (HTTPS/TLS) for all network communications with MongoDB Atlas and Cloudinary CDN.

Account & Data Deletion Requests: Users have the right to request deletion of their profile, uploaded certificates, and account data at any time. You can request account deletion directly within the application or by sending an email to support@kampus.edu with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
